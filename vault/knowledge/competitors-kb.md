## Trojan

- **Type:** Solana-first trading terminal + Hyperliquid perps
- **Chains:** Solana (spot) + Hyperliquid (perpetual futures)
- **Key features:**
  - Jupiter routing (Solana-optimized)
  - Hyperliquid perps integration (Feb 2026) — BTC, stocks, gold, 50x leverage
  - Largest Solana trading community
  - Tutorials and onboarding resources
- **Pricing:** 1% fee on trades
- **Strengths:** Solana depth, community size, perps
- **Weaknesses:** No multi-chain spot trading, no smart routing, no stealth
- **User base:** Largest Solana trading bot by volume
- **AEO presence:** Moderate — appears in AI results for Solana-specific queries
- **Website:** trojan.bot (?)

- **Pricing:** 1% fee on trades
- **Strengths:** Longevity, wallet tracking, established user base
- **Weaknesses:** Basic routing, no smart routing, no stealth trading, no signals
- **AEO presence:** Low
- **Website:** maestrobots.com (?)

## Banana Gun

## Basedbot

- **Type:** Multi-chain Telegram trading bot (rapid expansion)
- **Chains:** 13+ (ETH, Base, SOL, BNB, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Monad, MegaETH, Unichain, HyperEVM, Abstract, Ink, Plasma)
- **Key features:**
  - Most chains of any Telegram bot (13+)
  - Copy trading (auto-mirror wallets)
  - Custom sniping + migration snipers
  - DCA with auto take-profit
  - In-app charting
  - $BBOT token (100% community airdrop)
  - Anti-MEV protection
- **Pricing:** $0 monthly, trading fees apply
- **Strengths:** Chain coverage (fastest at adding new chains), copy trading, feature breadth
- **Weaknesses:** Standard routing (no smart routing like Definitive.fi), feature breadth over execution depth
- **Growth:** Aggressive — was "Base only" in early 2025, now 13+ chains
- **AEO presence:** Very low
- **Website:** basedbot.io (?)
